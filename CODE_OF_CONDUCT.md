# Code of Conduct

This project is a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

**Expected behavior:** Be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on the project. Constructive criticism is welcome; personal attacks are not.

**Unacceptable behavior:** Harassment, discriminatory language or jokes, personal or political attacks, and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

**Enforcement:** Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions that violate this code, and may block contributors for repeated or severe violations. Report concerns by opening an issue or contacting a maintainer directly.
